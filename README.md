## What it reviews

The reviewer helps a Chartered Accountant perform a structured pre-sign review of uploaded financial statements. Depending on the information accessible in the uploaded file, checks include:

- Mathematical accuracy, totals, subtotals and arithmetic footing
- Links between the Statement of Financial Position, Profit or Loss, Changes in Equity and Cash Flows
- Opening and closing balance continuity
- Fixed asset, depreciation and other supporting schedules
- Note references, headings, reporting periods and dates
- Disclosure completeness, wording, grammar and rounding differences

It is designed to report **actionable exceptions**, rather than filling the report with checks that passed.

## Commands

| Command | Report language |
| --- | --- |
| `/audit` | English |
| `/auditbd` | Bangla-English |

Upload a financial statement in PDF or Excel format, then issue the relevant command. The workflow generates a DOCX pre-review report with findings, suggested corrective actions and a 100-mark scorecard.

## Important limitations

This is an AI-assisted preliminary review aid. It is **not an audit opinion, assurance engagement, certification or substitute for audit procedures**. The reviewer must identify inaccessible, unreadable or unverified information and must not claim checks that the available file content does not support. Final professional judgment and responsibility remain with the signing Chartered Accountant.
